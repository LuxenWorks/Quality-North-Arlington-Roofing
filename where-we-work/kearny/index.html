Kearny, NJ

Roofing in Kearny, NJ: Two- and Three-Family Roofs

Call (551) 210-7708 for a time

Kearny's housing is dense and layered: more than four in ten homes sit in two-, three- or four-unit buildings, so one roof often covers several households. For a stain, a loose gutter or a lifted run of shingles, start with roof repair or gutters and downspouts, then call (551) 210-7708 with the address and what you can see.

About the homes

In the Census Bureau's 2020-2024 estimate, Kearny's median home, among 15,367, dates from 1951, give or take 3 years. Detached houses number 4,618, attached houses 853, two-unit buildings 4,070 and buildings with three or four units 2,427. Rented homes account for 8,418 of the 14,751 occupied ones, or 57.1 percent, so plenty of Kearny households will begin with a message to the landlord or property manager.

Around town

Kearny's edge along Route 7

North Arlington lies along Kearny's northern edge, where the Belleville Turnpike (Route 7) runs. Town Hall is at 402 Kearny Ave, and the construction code enforcement page points its map to the annex at 410 Kearny Ave. Of the other three towns here, Belleville and Lyndhurst also share a border with Kearny.

What neighbors ask

What kinds of buildings does Kearny have?

Out of 15,367 homes in the Census Bureau's 2020-2024 estimate, 4,618 are detached houses, 853 attached, 4,070 two-unit buildings and 2,427 have three or four units. The median home was built in 1951, give or take 3 years, which is worth remembering when a roof's age comes up.

Does Kearny have a downspout rule?

Kearny's stormwater page lists an illicit-connection ordinance but says nothing about roof leaders or downspouts. Where a downspout may drain is a question for the town office, and it is worth settling before anyone redirects one.

Who can call about a Kearny roof?

If you own the building, or manage it for an owner, call (551) 210-7708 with the address, the roof's age and what you can see from the ground. A renter's first move is a message to the landlord or property manager. Danger comes before any of that: dial 911.

What goes wrong here

Trouble spots on a Kearny roof

One roof, several households.

In a two-, three- or four-unit building, a stain in one flat may come from a roof section over another. A dated photo of the stain, passed to the landlord or property manager, helps the roofer find the right slope.

Gutters and where the water ends up.

Rutgers' rule of thumb, 500 gallons off 800 square feet of roof for each inch of rain, is a lot of water for a side yard, so it is worth checking where each downspout ends. Kearny's stormwater page lists an illicit-connection ordinance but nothing on roof leaders, so ask the town. See gutters and downspouts.

Flat sections.

A flat roof over a rear addition or a porch drains slowly by design, and water still standing two days after a rain is the sign to look; see flat roofs.
