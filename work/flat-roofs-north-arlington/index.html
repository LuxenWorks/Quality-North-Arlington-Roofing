When it cannot wait

Water that stays after the sky clears

A flat roof is meant to shed water slowly, not to keep it. The Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association calls it ponding when water is still there 48 hours after the rain stops, and it gives 1/4 inch of fall per foot as the minimum slope for drainage. Freeze and thaw can wear a membrane where water stays.

Standing puddles past that mark, a stain on the ceiling under the flat section, or a membrane that blisters, splits or lifts at an edge: any of them is enough to dial (551) 210-7708 and book a look this week.

What the work involves

Where a flat roof gives way first

Flat and low-slope sections, such as a rear addition, a porch or the top of a bay, fail at the same few places: the seams between sheets of membrane, the corners and edges, the spots where a pipe or a wall rises through, and the drain or scupper where water is supposed to leave.

Under the Department of Energy's definition, low-slope means a pitch no steeper than 2:12, and the usual coverings are single-ply membranes, built-up roofs and modified bitumen. Each is installed differently, so a seam repair on one is not the same job as on another.

Stop and get help first if

  • Water has stood on the flat section for days and the ceiling underneath is drooping: close that room off, and call 911 if the ceiling starts to come down.
  • The surface feels soft or springy underfoot: stay off the roof and keep others off it, since the deck below may be rotted.
  • Wires cross close to the parapet or the roof edge: stay off the roof, and phone the utility or 911 if one is sagging or sparking.
  • A ceiling light drips or buzzes after rain: leave the room, leave the switch alone, and call 911 from a dry spot.

Close to home

The borough's mix includes 329 attached houses, 1,838 two-unit buildings and 871 with three or four units, per the 2020-2024 Census estimate. Additions, porches and bays often carry a flat section, and a walk around the outside of the house shows whether yours does.

Teterboro's July normals pair an average high of 87.0 degrees with 1.5 days that reach an inch of rain. The Department of Energy says a standard roof surface can climb to 150 degrees or higher under afternoon sun, which is hard on a membrane that then has to take a summer downpour.

What moves the price

What drives the price of a flat roof job

A seam repair, a section replacement and a full re-cover are three different prices. The covering sets the base: single-ply sheets, built-up layers and modified bitumen each go on differently. After that come the drains and edges, the number of pipes and vents poking through, and whether wet insulation has to be pulled out. The Department of Energy's repair brief says soaked insulation has to be removed.

If water ponds, slope and drainage enter the conversation too, since the trade group ties standing water to slope, drains and scuppers.

Paperwork pointer

Paperwork on a flat section

The state's maintenance alert deals with detached homes holding one or two families, and a flat section often belongs to an attached house or a larger building. Whether a job needs a permit is the town office's call; the rules page names the office in each town.
