When it cannot wait

Water going over the edge instead of down

Three signs say a gutter job cannot wait for spring. Water slops over the front lip in a normal rain. A run has pulled away from the fascia, or a corner has opened up. Or a wet streak shows on the wall below the gutter, or the basement wall is damp after storms.

Each means roof water is going somewhere it was never meant to. Call __PHONE_DISPLAY__ and ask for a look at the run, and name the downspout nearest the wet spot.

What the work involves

The parts of a gutter run

A run has four parts worth knowing by name. The gutter itself tilts slightly toward the outlet. Hangers hold it to the fascia board. The outlet drops into a downspout, and the downspout ends in an elbow or an extension that points water away from the wall.

The EPA's page on redirecting downspouts cautions that disconnecting one is not right for every property and that local regulations apply, so check before changing where one drains.

Stop and get help first if

  • A gutter, downspout or ladder is touching a power line: do not touch the metal, keep everyone clear, and call 911 or the utility.
  • Water is seeping into a basement with a panel or outlets nearby: do not step in it, leave the area, and call 911 if it keeps rising.
  • Heavy icicles or a loaded gutter are pulling away from the fascia: keep people, pets and cars out from beneath it, and do not knock the ice down from a ladder.
  • A ladder is the only way to reach the gutter: do not climb on frozen ground or with wet gloves, and leave the cleaning to a roofer.

Close to home

Rutgers Water Resources puts the yield of an 800-square-foot roof at about 500 gallons per inch of rain. Ida's 9.02 inches at the North Arlington station (1-2 September 2021) works out to roughly 4,500 gallons from that much roof, by simple multiplication rather than any published measurement.

The 2026 Bergen County hazard mitigation plan maps 344.85 of North Arlington's 1,619.78 acres, about 21 percent, in the 1 percent annual-chance floodplain. With so much low ground in the borough, it matters where a downspout empties.

No downspout rule turned up in North Arlington's borough pages, and Kearny's stormwater page mentions an illicit-connection ordinance but nothing about roof leaders. Before changing where a downspout empties, ask the town office.

What moves the price

How a gutter quote adds up

Length of run and number of corners come first. The material matters next: aluminum, steel and copper each carry a different price and a different set of edge details. Then come the downspout count, the height of the eave, whether the fascia boards behind are sound enough to hold new hangers, and whether the run is being cleaned, repaired, re-pitched or replaced.

A cleaning is the smallest job in the group. A replacement of a long run on a three-story house is the largest.

Paperwork pointer

Where downspouts may drain

The state's list of ordinary maintenance includes work on exterior gutters and leaders, whether repair, replacement or a brand-new installation, with no permit application. Whether a town limits where a downspout discharges is a separate question; the rules page lists the offices to ask.
