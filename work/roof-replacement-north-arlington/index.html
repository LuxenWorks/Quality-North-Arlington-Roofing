When it cannot wait

Signs the old roof is done

Shingles that curl at the edges, shed grit into the downspouts or crack when touched have reached the end. So has a roof with leaks in two or three places that keep coming back after patches. A sagging ridge line, or daylight showing through the boards in the attic, means the frame under the covering needs a look soon, not next season.

Seeing two or more of these is reason to dial __PHONE_DISPLAY__ and ask when a roofer can come. A winter of waiting can turn a roof job into a roof, insulation and ceiling job.

What the work involves

A tear-off, from the top layer down

Replacement starts with the old shingles and nails coming off. The deck gets checked for soft or stained boards, and anything bad is cut out and replaced. Underlayment goes down next, flashing goes in at the chimney, walls and valleys, and the new shingles start at the eave with a starter strip and climb toward the ridge.

FEMA's high-wind fact sheet on asphalt shingles asks for six nails per shingle along the eave, roofing nails rather than staples, and underlayment in every case. Those three details are easy to ask about.

Stop and get help first if

  • Shingles or sheathing are lifting off in a high wind: keep everyone away from the house and the yard below, and call 911 if debris has struck a person.
  • A slope is sagging or the ceilings below are bowing: leave the top floor and call 911.
  • Loose material, a ladder or a pole is leaning on a service wire at the roofline: back off, keep others away, and phone PSE&G on 1-800-436-7734, or 911 if anyone is hurt.
  • A tear-off is happening overhead: keep children, pets and cars away from the eaves, and do not stand under the drip line while debris is coming down.

Close to home

The borough is not all single-family. The Census Bureau's 2020-2024 estimate counts 2,457 detached houses, 1,838 two-unit buildings and 871 with three or four units, out of about 6,900 homes. A two- or three-family house puts one roof over several households, which is worth keeping in mind when a tear-off date is set.

The state's codes page records 17 August 2026 as the adoption date for the New Jersey versions of the 2024 International Building and Residential Codes. The maintenance alert is firm that the ordinary-maintenance label gives no pass on code compliance, so it is fair to ask the roofer which edition the job follows.

What moves the price

Why two roofs of the same size price differently

Square footage sets the amount of material, and almost everything else sets the labor. Pitch, the number of stories, the layers to be torn off, rotted deck boards found underneath, the count of chimneys, skylights and valleys, and the room for a dumpster in a narrow driveway all move the number. The shingle line chosen moves it too, as does adding an ice barrier along the eaves.

A written quote can list these items separately, which makes two quotes easier to compare line by line.

Paperwork pointer

Paperwork for a full replacement

A detached one- or two-family home can have its roof covering replaced in full without a permit application, according to the state's maintenance alert, which also says that the label is no exemption from the building code. Where the alert stops, and which office to ask in each town, is on the local rules page.
