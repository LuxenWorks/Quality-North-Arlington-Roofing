What happens to your details, in a letter

This letter walks through what becomes of the details that come from a visit to qualitynortharlingtonroofing.com or from a call to the number shown there. The business behind it is Quality North Arlington Roofing, 70 Ridge Rd, North Arlington, NJ 07031, which puts homeowners and landlords in touch with a local roofing contractor by phone.

Who you are dealing withQuality North Arlington Roofing connects people who need roof work in North Arlington, Kearny, Lyndhurst or Belleville, New Jersey, with a local contractor. It does no roofing itself. The contractor who answers the call does the work, under its own agreement with the homeowner or landlord.

What a visit leaves behindWhoever hosts the pages keeps routine logs: an IP address, the browser and device in use, which pages were opened and when. Analytics tools may also count visits, taps on the phone number and where a call came from, using cookies or similar technology. Nothing on the pages asks a visitor to type anything, so a name or street address reaches the business only if a caller speaks it.

What happens on a callThe phone number shown on the pages runs through a call-tracking service. That service logs the caller's number, when the call happened, how long it lasted and which page or source brought the caller there. Calls are recorded only after a spoken notice, given at the start, says so. Whatever a caller mentions, from a name or street address to how the roof looks, becomes part of the call record.

Who gets the detailsThe contractor who answers is told what the caller says, enough to set up a visit. The call-tracking service, the analytics tools and the web host handle details on the way through. None of it is posted publicly or sold.

Cookies and browser signalsCookies can be blocked in the browser settings by anyone who wants less tracking. A Global Privacy Control signal sent by the browser is treated as a request that details not be sold or shared, and the pages behave the same either way.

Asking about a callTo find out what is held about a call, or to have it deleted, phone the number on the pages and ask. An answer comes within 45 days.

ChildrenThese pages are meant for adults who own or look after property, and nobody under 16 is their audience. The business does not seek details from children.
